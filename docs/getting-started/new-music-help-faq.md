# New Music borwsing changes in 9.0
Release 9.0 intorduced changes to browsing your music by New Music added to your LMS music library and offers and additional new browse menu option for viewing music that was most recently updated.  This page is intended to provide clarification about this change and answer server frequently asked questions about this change.

## What's new with browsing by New Music
Selecting Browse by New Music will display a list of albums sorted by the actual date that the album was first scanned into your LMS library.
